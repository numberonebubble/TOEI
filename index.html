Odd Earth Institute
Things that should exist, but don’t:

Each idea animates as: globe → idea text → globe. Scroll or use the buttons. (Press Enter to replay the current transition.)

mobility × behavior
Emotionally aware GPS

It’s weird that navigation optimizes minutes but ignores mood. A GPS that detects rising stress and quietly chooses routes that reduce friction — without turning your commute into a lecture.

diagnostics
30-second infection detection

A tiny, fast, first-pass signal for “something infectious is likely happening” should be as normal as a thermometer. Not definitive—just fast triage you can repeat over time.

food × field tools
Caffeine quantifying coffee cups

A disposable lid or cup insert that estimates caffeine content on the spot. It’s weird that we still treat caffeine as folklore when it’s one of the most consumed psychoactive molecules on Earth.

nutrition × measurement
Nutrient profiling device

A pocketable way to estimate basic nutrient signals (macro-ish + key micronutrient proxies) from common foods. Not perfect—good enough to teach your intuition with real numbers.

clinical tools
Vein finders for neonatal patients

Neonatal vein access should not depend on luck and experience alone. A tiny, affordable vein finder designed specifically for neonates feels like an obvious missing object.

care systems
Universal fall detection for old age homes

Fall detection shouldn’t require buying a particular watch or brand ecosystem. It should be universal, ambient, and opt-in—working across rooms, beds, and walkers.

living sensors
Desk plants that diagnose cancer

It’s weird that plants aren’t part of diagnostics culture. A desk plant that responds (and is measured) alongside human biomarkers—becoming a living, off-body sensing companion.

security × bio
Bio-secure safes and vaults

A safe that authenticates via biological liveness (not just a fingerprint) should be commonplace: stress patterns, pulse presence, multi-signal confirmation—security that’s hard to spoof.

mobility × retrofit
Stick-on parking sensors for any vehicle

Parking sensors shouldn’t be “new-car privilege.” A universal stick-on kit that feels factory-fitted, installs in minutes, and just works—why is this not everywhere?

agri × forecasting
Crop-yield prediction from saplings

A way to estimate yield potential early—from sapling traits + simple environment signals—would change planning, logistics, and risk. It’s weird this isn’t a farmer’s default tool.
